One sentence, at most 280 characters, that brings back a childhood memory — written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-nostalgic-a-childhood-625d3363).

## The line

> When I was seven, my grandmother shelled peas on the porch while the radio crackled, and I still believe the whole world smelled of rain on hot dust and rice steaming in her kitchen.

## How it works

`main.py` is deterministic (no network, no randomness) and writes the single sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (and to `entry.txt` beside it for local verification).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
